# Generic AI-generated design patterns to avoid

Avoid these by default. They are not absolute bans: use one when the brand, product, existing design system, accessibility or usability justifies it, and say why.

1. **Harsh gradients.** Skip excessive, decorative, high-contrast gradients.
2. **Lucide icons by default.** Reuse the project's icon assets or pick the most appropriate icon set.
3. **Pure white backgrounds everywhere.** Choose backgrounds deliberately; white is fine when it fits.
4. **Rainbow coloring.** No arbitrary, unrelated accent colors.
5. **Drop shadows everywhere.** Use elevation only to communicate hierarchy or separation.
6. **Three feature cards in every row.** Let content and hierarchy decide the layout.
7. **Emojis as UI icons.** Use proper icons unless emojis suit the product.
8. **Liquid glass, glassmorphism, blur.** No unnecessary translucent surfaces.
9. **Em dashes in UI and marketing copy.**
10. **Inter, Geist or Space Grotesk by default.** Choose type for brand, readability and language support (here: the user's choice, else system font).
11. **Decorative colored left stripes.** Only for status or meaningful hierarchy.
12. **Fake testimonials, ratings, logos, reviews, metrics.** Never invent them.
13. **Bento grids everywhere.** Use varied grids only when content benefits.
14. **Decorative terminal windows** or fake code snippets to look technical.
15. **"It's not X, it's Y" copywriting.** Avoid generic contrast formulas.
16. **Checkmark bullets everywhere.** Use markers that suit the content.
17. **Exactly three pricing tiers.** Mirror the real pricing model.
18. **Generic mockups instead of real demos.** Prefer real product evidence; never present a mockup as a live product.
19. **Soft rounded corners on everything.** Use a deliberate radius scale per component.
20. **Purple and black by default.** Palette follows the brand and the user's choice.
21. **Missing skeleton loaders.** Add loading states where they improve perceived performance; not mandatory for every operation.
22. **Decorative radial orbs.** No glowing circles or blurred background blobs as filler.
23. **Decorative dot grids.** No technical grids as generic filler.
24. **Sparkle icons everywhere.** AI symbols only when they communicate a real feature.
25. **Animated arrows everywhere.** Animate only when motion conveys direction or interaction.
26. **Missing Terms of Service.** Include when required by the product, business model, law or app store.
27. **Missing Privacy Policy.** Include when applicable, accurate to real data practices, with app store data disclosures.
28. **Unnecessary hover or press animations.** Respect reduced motion. (Hover and press effects on controls are already disabled by this skill.)
29. **Neon colors by default.** Avoid excess saturation and glow unless intentional brand elements.
30. **Generic pastel palettes by default.** Do not pick pastels just because they look modern.
